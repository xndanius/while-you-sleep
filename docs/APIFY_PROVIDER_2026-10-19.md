## Bounds and evidence

Four six-hour Vilnius collection buckets provide at most 100 Reddit, 100 X, 50 Indie Hackers and 50 Product Hunt reserved ingestion slots per local day. Failures consume the slots. A duplicate bucket cannot make a second paid request. Existing official X reservations count against the Apify X allowance too. Official credentials suppress the corresponding Apify platform; until an official adapter supplies trusted raw discussion evidence, health explicitly says `official_primary_unavailable_until_validated`. xAI model summaries are never represented as primary quotes.

Each paid request has a maximum $0.20 provider-side `maxTotalChargeUsd`, plus `maxItems`, bounded actor input limits, 120-second actor timeout and no automatic restart. The immutable monetary reservation is committed and pushed before the POST. An uncertain POST is never retried. Authenticated owner `usageTotalUsd` can still be preliminary after terminal completion, so no runtime usage observation releases any part of a reservation. Full caps, including failures, count against the monthly $10 limit. [Apify documents the hard request cap](https://docs.apify.com/api/v2/actors-runs-post), and [the owner cost and preliminary completion figures](https://docs.apify.com/api/v2/actor-run-get).

Only five post fields are stored: URL, community, actual date if supplied, title and a literal excerpt of at most 200 characters. Author names, account handles, profiles, cookies, contacts and raw actor output are discarded. X URLs use `/i/status/ID`; Reddit URLs use `/comments/ID`. Separate immutable control receipts bind the exact allowlisted actor, actor run, dataset, reservation and pool content hashes. The checker independently opens those receipts and captures without a token, retains the original capture timestamp, and accepts a capture for at most eight hours. It does not claim a local receipt reread is a new public-site fetch. Where the actor omits an actual post date, the discussion adapter rejects that item.

Two consecutive failures or empty batches switch the platform to its one backup and mark probation. `data/apify/status.json` is the current public operational summary; immutable receipts retain the history. The existing watchdog can include this health in its repair diagnosis.
